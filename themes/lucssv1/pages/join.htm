Joining

Members of Lancaster University Students' Union or the School of Computing and Communications automatically qualify for free membership to LUCSS for the duration of their study or tenure. Follow the link below to officially register:

Register

Lifetime membership

If you're interested in becoming a lifetime member of LUCSS; for full access to our resources and network, then you can register here. Please note that a one-time fee of £5 applies.

Lifetime membership can be awarded for significant contributions to the society, this may be during your membership as a student or academic staff at the university or through sponsorship.
Benefits
  • Access to our newsletter with updates on events, job opportunities, and more;
  • Access to our Chat Server; and
  • Discount prices on special events.
