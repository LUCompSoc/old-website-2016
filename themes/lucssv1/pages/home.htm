Hello, World!

Lancaster University Computer Science Society (LUCSS or LUCompSoc) is a group of like-minded individuals all of whom share a common interest in the realm of computing. We hold hackathons, opportunities to network with industry, invite academics and professionals to give talks, provide either course-specific revision or general interest workshops, and more!

If you're interested in participating, be sure to join us at our weekly meetup every Thursday at 6pm!
